After-Hours Elevator Access

At an occupied condo building, residents needed a way to let visitors use the elevator after hours when new wiring was impractical. At SmartONE, I brought CDVI into the integration, documented requirements for the software team and security contractor, and coordinated live testing through client acceptance. The shipped app feature lets residents release elevator access temporarily for guests and delivery drivers.
